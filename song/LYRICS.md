# Kunwari Okay

Original OPM hugot ballad · Key of D (final chorus modulates up to E) · 80 BPM · 4/4

Theme: the panganay, the "malakas" friend, the one who always replies "haha" in the
group chat but cries into the pillow at night. Pretending to be okay, and finally
admitting you're not.
