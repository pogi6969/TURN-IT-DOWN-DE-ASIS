# Matapos an Bagyo (After the Storm)

Original Bikol hugot ballad · Key of D, final chorus up to E · 80 BPM · same chords as "Kunwari Okay"

**[Verse 1]**
Makusog an uran sa atop *(the rain is strong on the roof)*
Si Nanay nagdadasal sa may kandila *(Mom prays by the candle)*
Mayo nin kuryente, mayo nin signal *(no power, no signal)*
Ako sana asin an sakong luha *(just me and my tears)*
Sabi ninda, sanay na kita sa bagyo *(they say we're used to storms)*
Taga-Bikol, dai nagtatangis *(Bicolanos don't cry)*
Pero an bagyo sa laog kan puso ko *(but the storm inside my heart)*
Mayo nin nakakaaram *(no one knows)*

**[Pre-Chorus]**
Kaya kun maghapot ka kun kumusta na ako *(so if you ask how I am)*
Sasabihon ko man giraray, "Marhay man ako" *(I'll say again, "I'm fine")*

**[Chorus]**
Matapos an bagyo, masirang giraray an aldaw *(after the storm, the sun will shine again)*
Maski ngunyan, garo dai ko na kaya *(even if right now I feel I can't take it)*
Itatangis ko sana an gabos na kulog *(I'll just cry out all the pain)*
Dangan matindog giraray ako *(then I'll stand up again)*
Matapos an bagyo, dai na ako matatakot *(after the storm, I won't be afraid)*
Ta an uran, dai man pirmi *(because the rain isn't forever)*
Kun ika man nasa bagyo ngunyan *(if you're in a storm right now too)*
Kapot sako, dai taka babayaan *(hold on to me, I won't leave you)*

**[Verse 2]**
Si Tatay yaon sa abroad *(Dad is abroad)*
Nakikita ko sana sa video call *(I only see him on video call)*
Ika na nagsabing dai ka mahali *(you who said you'd never leave)*
Ika man palan an enot na nagbaya *(turns out you were first to go)*
Nabasa kan uran an satong litrato *(the rain soaked our photo)*
Garo kita, nawara na sana *(like us, just gone)*
Dakul na bagyo an nag-abot sa sakong buhay *(many storms have hit my life)*
Alagad nakatindog pa man giraray *(but I'm still standing)*

**[Pre-Chorus]** · **[Chorus]** *(repeat)*

**[Bridge]**
Bako ako maluya, pagal sana *(I'm not weak, just tired)*
Bako ako nagsusuko, nagpapahingalo sana *(not giving up, just resting)*
Kun sa aga, magmamata pa ako *(if tomorrow I still wake up)*
Boot sabihon, kaya ko pa ini *(it means I can still do this)*

**[Final Chorus]** *(up to E)*
Matapos an bagyo, dai na ako magtatago *(after the storm, I won't hide anymore)*
Bako ako marhay, asin marhay man iyan *(I'm not okay, and that's okay)*
Itatangis ko an gabos na kulog *(I'll cry out all the pain)*
Sa aga, maliwanag giraray an langit *(tomorrow the sky will be bright again)*
Kaya ika, na nakangirit man *(so you, who's smiling too)*
Maski pagal, maski makulog *(even tired, even hurting)*
Dai ka nagsosolo digdi *(you're not alone here)*
Kukugoson taka, totoo *(I'll hug you, for real)*

**[Outro]**
Kumusta ka? ...An totoo? *(How are you? ...Really?)*
